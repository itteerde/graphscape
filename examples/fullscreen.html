Full screen

The view switches in and out of full screen from the Full button inside the graph, from the page button below, or with the F key. Esc leaves it. Where the browser has no Fullscreen API (iPhone Safari) the view fills the viewport instead.

windowed

Hover a node for details. Diamonds are team leads (shape and sublabel on the node). Ctrl-click a node with a link to open it, double-click works too. Drag with the left button to pan, wheel to zoom.
